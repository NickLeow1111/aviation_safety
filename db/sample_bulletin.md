CAAS SAFETY BULLETIN 2026-07
Runway incursion risk at Singapore Changi (WSSS)

1. Background
Between January and October 2026 the safety data mart recorded six runway
incursion events at WSSS, all of them involving ground vehicles or tow crews
entering the active runway without a clearance. None resulted in injury.

2. Findings
- Four of the six events occurred during taxiway works on the northern parallel
  taxiway, when vehicles were routed across RWY 02L.
- Two events involved tow crews crossing the CAT II hold point without notifying
  Tower.
- The mean elapsed time between the hold short line breach and the Tower call was
  11 seconds; in both tow-crew events the Tower initiated the call, not the driver.

3. Local action required
- Airside operators must brief all drivers on the revised taxi routing before
  their first shift after 1 November 2026.
- Towers are to record every hold short line breach in the safety data mart within
  24 hours, including those resolved without a go-around.
- Tow crews are to make a mandatory radio call on channel 3 before crossing RWY 02L.

4. Known limitations of the data
The occurrence table records the date of an event but not the time of day, and it
records the location as an aerodrome code rather than coordinates. Any time-of-day
series in this bulletin is derived and must not be quoted as recorded fact.
